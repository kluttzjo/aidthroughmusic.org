Holiday Concert featuring "Known by Name" (formerly J209)
Our annual holiday concert is the cornerstone of AMP Productions and the event that inspired our organization from the very beginning. This year, we are thrilled to welcome Known by Name (formerly J209), an award-winning seven-member a cappella vocal band renowned for their powerful harmonies, dynamic performances, and engaging stage presence.

The evening will feature an opening set from Strawberry Serenade, Lebanon High School's own contemporary a cappella group, followed by a full concert by Known by Name. Members of the school's Chamber Choir will also join Known by Name on stage as a backup gospel choir — giving local students the rare and unforgettable experience of sharing the stage with a professional award-winning group.

📍 Lebanon High School Auditorium
🎭 Matinee: 2:00 PM  ·  Evening: 7:00 PM
