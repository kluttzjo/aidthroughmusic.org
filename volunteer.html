Get Involved

Volunteer with AMP

Be part of the music. Join our volunteer team and help us create unforgettable experiences for our community.

Why Volunteer

More Than Just Helping Out

🎵
Be Part of the Music
Work behind the scenes of live performances and experience the energy of a concert from the inside out.
🌱
Give Back
Every volunteer hour directly supports performing arts programs at Lebanon High School and our broader community mission.
🤝
Build Connections
Meet like-minded people who share your passion for music, community, and making a difference.
⭐
Grow Your Skills
Gain hands-on experience in event production, hospitality, technical operations, and more.
Opportunities

Volunteer Roles

🎟
Event Staff
Serve as ushers, ticket takers, and greeters — the friendly face that welcomes our audience and ensures a smooth event experience for everyone.
🎛
Sound & Lighting Tech
Support our technical team with sound and lighting setup and operation. Experience preferred but enthusiasm welcomed — training provided where possible.
🎤
Stage Crew
Help with stage setup, teardown, and equipment management before and after performances. Physical work but hugely impactful.
🌟
Hospitality
Provide backstage support for our performers — helping ensure artists are comfortable, prepared, and ready to give their best performance.
📸
Photography & Videography
Capture the magic of our events through photos and video. Content created by our volunteers helps share AMP Productions' story with the world.
🍿
Concessions
Operate our concessions area during events — a fun, high-energy role that puts you right in the middle of the audience experience.
