Founding Members

Be part of where it all began. Every person who supports AMP Productions in our inaugural year of 2026 earns Founding Member status — a lifetime honor that can never be earned again.

AMP Productions is a brand new 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to bringing exceptional live music experiences to the Willamette Valley while directly supporting performing arts programs at Lebanon High School.

In our inaugural year, every dollar given is more than a donation — it's a founding investment in the future of music and community. As a thank you for believing in us from day one, every founding member receives lifetime recognition and benefits that can never be earned again after December 31, 2026.
